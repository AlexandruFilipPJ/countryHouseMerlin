Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book? ➜

We recommend booking 4-6 weeks ahead for the best availability, but we accept last-minute bookings depending on the season.

What time are check-in and check-out? ➜

Check-in is at 3 PM and check-out at 11 AM. We can discuss flexible timing for special circumstances.

Are pets allowed? ➜

Yes! We welcome well-behaved pets. Please mention them when booking so we can prepare accordingly.

Can we prepare meals at the house? ➜

Absolutely. The kitchen is fully equipped, and cooking together is part of the experience. We can also arrange catering for special events.

What about music and celebrations? ➜

We love celebrations! Reasonable noise levels are welcome until 11 PM. Just let us know what you're planning.

What's the deposit and cancellation policy? ➜

Details will be provided with your booking inquiry. We work flexibly to accommodate your needs.
