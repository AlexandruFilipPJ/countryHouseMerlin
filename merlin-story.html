The Real Life of Merlin

Stories from entrepreneurship, events, travel, and the journey that led to this house.

The Journey Begins

From urban ambitions to country roots

This story didn't start in the countryside. It started in the middle of a city, with an idea and a determination to build something meaningful. For years, I've been deeply involved in entrepreneurship, launching brands, organizing events, and working with people who share the same passion for creating experiences worth remembering.

But somewhere along the way, I realized that the best moments—the ones that actually mattered—weren't always in boardrooms or at crowded venues. They were in homes, around tables, late into the night, with people you genuinely care about. That's when the idea for this house started to take shape.

Building Something Real

From concept to country living

The house wasn't built overnight. It took time, conversations with people I trust, and a clear vision of what I wanted to create. Not just a rental property, but a space where something genuine could happen. A place where teams could think differently, where friends could be friends again, where celebrations felt meaningful.

Every detail was considered with intention. The layout invites gatherings. The kitchen was built for cooking together. The living spaces encourage long conversations. The rooms are comfortable, but not ostentatious. This is a home designed for real life, not Instagram perfection.

The Art of Creating Experiences

What I've learned about bringing people together

My background in events and brand building has taught me something fundamental: the best experiences happen when people feel welcomed and understood. Not overproduced. Not trying too hard. Just genuine hospitality and thoughtful curation.

Country House by Merlin reflects that philosophy. Whether you're bringing your team for a retreat, celebrating a milestone with close friends, or simply looking for a weekend away from the noise, the house adapts to what you need. We're here to facilitate, not to dictate.

Travels and Perspective

How the world shapes your vision

I've been fortunate to travel extensively, to see how different cultures create homes, how hospitality works across continents, and what makes a space feel like somewhere people want to be. Those experiences informed how I designed this house.

From the proportions of the rooms to the way light moves through them, from the kitchen setup to the outdoor spaces—everything draws inspiration from places and people that taught me what matters in a home. It's contemporary, but rooted. Urban, but grounded. A reflection of someone who understands both city life and the longing for escape.

Why This Matters to Me

The deeper purpose behind Country House by Merlin

At a time when we're all scattered—working remotely, scrolling endlessly, living transactionally—I wanted to create a counterpoint to that. A place where people actually show up, put their phones down, and spend real time together.

This house is a statement. It says that connection matters. That weekends matter. That the people in your life are worth your full attention. That some of the best ideas, celebrations, and memories happen when you step away from the usual noise and intentionally create space for what matters.

Whether you're here with your dearest friends, your team, or to celebrate something important, you're part of this philosophy. You're participating in something that pushes back against the ordinary.
